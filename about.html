LOG VIEWER

Client-side CSV parsing and plotting for ECU tuning logs.

What is it?

A client-side CSV/TXT log viewer for ECU log exports. Real-time graphs for RPM, boost, AFR, throttle, ignition, and related tuning channels. All parsing and rendering happens in your browser.

Key features

  • Signal Matrix: multi-axis grid with synced snap readouts
  • Correlation Lab: overlays, smoothing, highlighting, scaling, and time-window cropping
  • GR6 Gear Scope: shift points, torque intervention, and slip timelines
  • Session cache: switch views without re-uploading
  • Exports: PNG/SVG/ZIP/PDF reporting

Supported formats

ECU log CSV/TXT with a header row and time-series columns such as Time, RPM, Boost, AFR, Throttle, and Ignition.

Tech stack

Vanilla JS + HTML5 + CSS3. Plotly.js for charts, PapaParse for CSV. Optimized for large logs with no backend.

Privacy

Logs are processed locally for normal viewing workflows; your data stays on your device unless you explicitly use a cloud/archive feature.
